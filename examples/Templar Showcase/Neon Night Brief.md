# NEON NIGHT / CREATIVE BRIEF

**Project:** Afterimage · **Edition:** 01 · **Status:** ==IN MOTION==

![[Assets/neon-night.png]]

## Visual direction

Build a nocturnal story from **reflections, negative space, and electric color**. The frame should feel precise but lived-in: rain on concrete, a bicycle waiting in shadow, one luminous exit in the distance.

> [!abstract] Core idea
> The city becomes legible only after dark—through color, rhythm, and the spaces between signs.

### Palette system

| Role | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Void | `#070912` | Page and deep shadow |
| Signal | `#24d8ff` | Links, rules, key data |
| Pulse | `#ff2ea6` | Highlights and callouts |
| Haze | `#7d82a8` | Secondary copy |

```text
LIGHT  → reflection → geometry
SOUND  → distance   → pulse
STORY  → absence    → invitation
```

- [x] Lock the cyan/magenta balance
- [x] Keep typography sharp and restrained
- [ ] Cut a six-frame sequence from wide to intimate

> [!success] Creative constraint
> Use one bold gesture per frame. Let the rest breathe.
