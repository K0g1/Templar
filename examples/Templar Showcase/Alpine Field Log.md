# Alpine Field Log

**Route 04** · North basin to Mirror Lake · *Blue-hour departure*

![[Assets/alpine-blue-hour.webp]]

## The lake before sunrise

At 05:40 the valley was almost silent. Mist held close to the water while the peaks gathered the first cold light. A ==single red canoe== rested at the shoreline—our only bright landmark in a field of blue.

> [!info] Conditions
> **3°C** · light north wind · visibility improving · trail damp but clear

### Waypoints

1. **Pine Gate** — enter below the old fire road.
2. **Granite Shelf** — pause for the first basin view.
3. **Mirror Lake** — follow the western shore to camp.

| Leg | Distance | Elevation | Notes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Trailhead → Shelf | 3.8 km | +420 m | Steady switchbacks |
| Shelf → Lake | 2.1 km | +160 m | Exposed granite |
| Lake circuit | 4.4 km | ±90 m | Best light at dawn |

> [!warning] Turnaround rule
> If cloud closes over the eastern ridge before noon, return by the marked western trail.
