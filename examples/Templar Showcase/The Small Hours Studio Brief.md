# The small hours studio brief

**Project:** Afterimage · **Edition:** 02 · **Status:** in motion

![[Assets/neon-night.webp]]

## Visual direction

Build a nocturnal story from reflections, negative space, and electric color. Keep the frame precise but lived-in: rain on concrete, a bicycle waiting in shadow, one luminous exit in the distance. The city should feel close enough to touch and too large to explain.

> [!abstract] Working idea
> The city becomes legible after dark through color, rhythm, and the spaces between signs.

### Palette system

```text
LIGHT  -> reflection -> geometry
SOUND  -> distance   -> pulse
STORY  -> absence    -> invitation
```

### Frame notes

The first frame can be wide. Let the wet pavement carry the color while the buildings stay almost black. In the second frame, move closer to the doorway and leave the subject out of the center. A little empty space makes the light feel less staged.

- [x] Lock the cyan and magenta balance
- [x] Keep the type sharp and restrained
- [ ] Cut a six-frame sequence from wide to intimate

> [!success] Creative constraint
> Use one bold gesture per frame. Give the rest of the image somewhere to be quiet.

#### Note to self

Do not add another color just because the frame feels unfinished. Let the blue do its work.
