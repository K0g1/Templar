*Three stops above the inlet after a night of rain.*

## The path after weather

The rain stopped before dawn, but the trail kept its own version of the weather. Water ran down the rock steps. The fir needles held small beads of light. At the first bend, a red canoe sat below the trees with nobody around to claim it.

> [!info] Conditions
> 8°C · cloud lifting over the north ridge · trail damp but clear

### Three stops

| Stop | What changed | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pine Gate | The wind dropped | Wet bark smelled like tea |
| Granite Shelf | The clouds opened | The lake appeared in pieces |
| Mirror Lake | The light turned silver | A loon called once, then stopped |

### A route worth repeating

1. Leave the old fire road before the ground starts to rise.
2. Take the narrow shelf above the creek. It is slower, but the view arrives gradually.
3. Stay at the lake until the far ridge separates from the sky.

> [!warning] Small rule
> If the eastern ridge disappears before noon, turn back by the western trail. The shorter way is not the safer way in fog.
